Who do I call

Copy this whole folder into your website. Keep these files together:

  index.html
  styles.css
  app.js
  topics.json
  README.txt

Put the folder in your public files, for example:

  your-site/who-to-call/

Then open /who-to-call/ on that site. Any static host works. You do not
need Node after the files are on the site.

Make it yours:

  1. Edit topics.json, or open the page and use Add a problem.
  2. Change This city to your town name and phone.
  3. Download JSON and replace topics.json if you edited in the browser.

The sample city in topics.json is fake starter data.
Nothing on this page sends mail.
